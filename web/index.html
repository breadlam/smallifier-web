Smallifier

Shrinks a video clip to fit a size limit while keeping action and on-screen text as clear as the budget allows. Everything runs on your device; the video is never uploaded.

    How it works: one cheap analysis pass predicts, for every scene, how large and how good the encode would be at each
  resolution and quality setting. The budget is spent where it buys the most quality, then each chunk is encoded once
  with two-pass x264 to land on its share. Output is H.264 + Opus in MP4, which plays almost everywhere.
    
  
    x264 (GPL) and libopus are compiled to WebAssembly; decoding uses your browser's WebCodecs.
